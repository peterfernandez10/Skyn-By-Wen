Good to Know

Questions, answered

How should I prepare for my first facial?

Arrive with clean skin if possible, and avoid retinoids or exfoliants for 48 hours beforehand.

How long does permanent makeup last?

Lip blush and brow work typically last 1–3 years, depending on skin type and sun exposure.

Why does altitude affect my skin?

Higher elevations bring stronger UV, drier air and more wind, which weaken the skin barrier. Our rituals are designed to restore it.

Is there any downtime?

Most facials have none. Permanent makeup may show mild redness for a day or two.

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly ask for 24 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel.
